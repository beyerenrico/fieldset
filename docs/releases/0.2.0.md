# form-schema-app 0.2.0: reports and offline filling (2026-10-18)

The second release adds reports and dashboards (M5) and makes the Field App work without a connection (M6).

## Highlights
- **Fill without a connection.** The Field App opens offline and keeps every entry on the device, also when the tab is closed or the phone runs out of battery. It sends the work exactly once when the connection returns, photos and signatures included. Numbers are assigned by the server at that moment ("number pending" until then).
- **Clear about what is where.** The header shows what is not yet synced and what needs a decision. Drafts changed elsewhere or refused by the office explain the next step. Settings shows storage use and offers "Sync now".
- **Safe on shared and lost devices.** Signing out or a remote wipe removes all local data. A device that an administrator revokes, or whose user is deactivated, wipes itself at its next contact. Work is never sent under another person's account.
- **Install it.** Add the Field App to the home screen; on iOS this also protects offline data from automatic deletion.
- **Reports.** A submission explorer per form shows columns from every version, with filters, search and decimal-exact money in each version's currency. Queued XLSX/CSV/PDF-ZIP exports notify you when ready. Saved reports show a table and a chart, can be scheduled as emails and can be pinned to the dashboard.
- **Dashboards.** Role-aware widgets (KPIs, submissions over time, workflow failures, review queue, drafts and published versions), a date-range filter and a short cache.

## Upgrade notes
- Run the migrations (`php artisan migrate --force`). The report indexes are built CONCURRENTLY.
- The Horizon configuration adds `supervisor-exports` for the `exports` queue. Restart Horizon after deploying.
- The Caddyfile now routes `/app/index.html` and `/form-preview/preview.html` through Laravel; rebuild the image.
- The Field App moves its local data into a new IndexedDB database on first start. Devices stay registered.
- Revoking a device now also signs that user out of their other sessions (QUESTIONS #9).

## Known limitations
- Background Sync only wakes open tabs; on iOS the app syncs when it is opened (QUESTIONS #13).
- No SSO and no canvas builder yet (planned for 1.0). Decisions waiting for human review are listed in `docs/agent/QUESTIONS.md`.
